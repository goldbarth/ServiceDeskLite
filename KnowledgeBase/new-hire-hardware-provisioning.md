## Standard Loadout

Every new hire receives a standard loadout: a laptop imaged with the current
corporate build, a docking station, two external monitors, and a headset.
Engineering roles additionally get a 32 GB RAM upgrade and admin rights scoped to
their own machine. Order the loadout at least five business days before the start
date so imaging and asset tagging complete in time.

## Accounts and Access

The directory account is created by HR onboarding, not IT. IT provisions
group membership, email, and VPN once the account exists. Do not create a
shadow account when the directory account is late — chase HR onboarding instead,
because a shadow account breaks single sign-on and has to be merged later.

## First-Day Checklist

On day one, confirm the laptop boots the corporate build, the user can sign in
with their directory password, email loads, and the VPN "Remote-Full" profile
connects. File any failure as a `hardware` or `access` ticket linked to the
onboarding record so the start-date SLA is tracked.
